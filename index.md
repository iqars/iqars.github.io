### The Boundaries of Quantum Computational Advantage

#### A. Quantum Complexity Theory

- **[QMA Has Perfect Completeness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13032)** (2026)<br>
  <span class="paper-summary">Resolves a longstanding open problem in quantum complexity theory by proving QMA = QMA_1.</span>

#### B. Efficient Classical Simulation of Quantum Circuits

- **[Efficiently Simulable Quantum Circuits with Large Entanglement, Magic, and Non-Gaussianity via Code-Compiled Tensor Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08396)** (2026)<br>
  <span class="paper-summary">Identifies efficiently classically simulable circuits despite substantial entanglement, magic, and non-Gaussianity, challenging conventional indicators of quantum computational complexity.</span>
